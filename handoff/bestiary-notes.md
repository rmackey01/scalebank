# Bestiary naturalist notes (draft, Animator)

Species tags are set in Cormorant Garamond italic under the name; field notes in Cormorant italic, 2–3 lines on the 390 px sheet (each ≤ ~180 chars). Grounded in `dragons/design_bible.md`. These appear on **found** pages only; undiscovered tiles show the number and "???" and nothing else (no egg, no rarity, no hints).

| No. | Dragon | Species tag | Field note |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | Cinder | *Ash skink* | *Sly and quick, it hides wherever the coals are warmest. Startle one and the neck frill snaps open like a red fan; the candle-tip of its tail never quite goes out.* |
| 02 | Brine | *Tide wyrm* | *Calm and unhurried ashore, it sways as if a current still holds it. The barbels taste the air for rain. Only in deep water does it turn playful.* |
| 03 | Moss | *Grove warden* | *Sleepy and patient, it lets ferns and flowers take root along its bark-plated back. Field mice nest in the shell, and it is careful never to roll over.* |
| 04 | Inferno | *Forge bulwark* | *Stubborn, proud and fiercely loyal. The magma seams between its plates brighten with every breath, and a single thump of the club tail settles most arguments.* |
| 05 | Stormling | *Squall skipper* | *Never still. Four dragonfly wings beat out of step, and its ribbon tail streams in gusts no one else can feel. Rain beads on the wing veins and rolls off.* |
| 06 | Voidling | *Night prowler* | *Shy, curious and a thief of shiny things. It hides beneath its wing-cloak, and the freckles on its back trace constellations no astronomer has named.* |
| 07 | Tempest | *Storm sovereign* | *Aloof and restless, always watching the horizon. Static crawls along the lightning scars on its hide, and a thin arc leaps between its horns before a storm.* |

## Mutation suffixes (variant pages)

Shown on the variant sheet below the "{Mutation} mutation" badge, in Cormorant italic, one line. Generic so they work for every species.

| Code | Mutation | Flavour line |
|---|---|---|
| -M | Moonlit | *Hatched under a full moon; a cold silver light clings to it after dark.* |
| -O | Obsidian | *Shell-dark and glass-edged, as if it hatched from cooled volcanic stone.* |
| -G | Gilded | *A sheen of gold runs over its scales whenever it catches the light.* |
| -A | Aurora | *Faint northern lights ripple across its hide, never the same colour twice.* |

## Rules
- Rarity is the egg's tier only: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary. "Prize" is never a rarity; the dragon getting steps keeps its real rarity label and gets the warm-white glow + WALKING tag.
- Dex numbers: No. 01–07 for dragons, variants No. 01-M / 01-O / 01-G / 01-A etc.
